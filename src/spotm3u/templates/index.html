Local playlist converter

Exportify to M3U

Convert an Exportify playlist export into a local M3U playlist without giving this app access to your Spotify account.

How it works

  1. Open Exportify Open Exportify (opens in a new tab)
  2. Complete Spotify authentication in Exportify
  3. Press “Export All”
  4. Download the playlist export ZIP
  5. Upload that ZIP here
